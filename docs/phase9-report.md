# Phase 9 — Grounded runbook answers

Engineering scope complete: optional lab copilot assembles at most three authorized current chunks and proposes cited claims/next steps. No operational action, tool interface, agent loop or incident mutation is introduced. Keyword search remains the baseline/fallback. Hybrid/reranking is not adopted without a measured need.

A strict JSON contract requires source chunk IDs and exact 10–300-character quotations for every claim/proposal. Sources carry immutable runbook version/content hash and UTF-16 body spans. The application rejects forged IDs, fabricated quotations, unexpected fields/tools, malformed/truncated/unsafe output, invalid refusal/abstention and uncited drafts. The 12,000-character prompt and existing provider response/deadline budgets remain. Context is redacted for configured secret and credential patterns; returned context excerpts can therefore be sanitized while their hash/version/span refer to the original source. Redaction is defense in depth, not a promise to detect arbitrary sensitive text.

Authorization/publication/content are revalidated after generation; revoked membership or withdrawn/replaced sources discard the draft. No DB connection is held during inference. No sufficiently ranked source produces deterministic abstention without generation. The 0.2 cosine cutoff is a provisional lab heuristic, not calibrated confidence. Generation is enabled only when both AI/embedding lab flags and pinned digests are configured.

Validation: lint, typecheck, build and 36 unit checks pass; integration 81 total, 80 pass and one inherited native-lock skip. Seven dedicated RAG checks pass, including fixed-context strict/forged/false-claim/refusal/absence fixtures, end-to-end retrieval+generation fixture, cross-workspace denial, context credential redaction, source withdrawal/revocation during inference and disabled-AI fallback. Browser copilot flow passed with zero retries and deterministic provider: sources/version/span, unverified label and abstention after withdrawal. See phase9-evals.json and phase9-browser.json. Fixed-context and end-to-end lanes are explicitly distinct from live-model quality.

The finite literal oracle intentionally fails a false closure statement with a valid source quote while the structural contract accepts it. Citation resolution is enforced; semantic support is not established by citation presence. Quality targets remain the roadmap provisional 0.90 semantic-support/answerability targets; they have not been agreed as release gates through a calibrated live/human baseline. Under the new governance, that human/product acceptance component of Phase 9 DoD is consolidated at M2, rather than falsely marked complete or blocking the technical Phase 10 eval work. No production-quality claim or baseline promotion occurs.

Learning material accumulates for M2. Actual desktop generation, contradictory/poisoned/absent-context sampling and model usefulness remain in the one M2 bundle. Native Docker/CI acceptance inherited from Phase 8 remains unobserved in Work. Phase 10 may proceed within the authorized milestone; Phase 11 remains unauthorized.
