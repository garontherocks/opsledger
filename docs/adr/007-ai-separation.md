# ADR 007: AI After Deterministic Product
Status: Accepted baseline.

Problem: probabilistic outputs can obscure a weak product/QE foundation.
Decision: no model/provider/vector/agent/n8n dependency in core bootstrap. Introduce AI in Phase 6 after Phase 5 gate, direct mechanics before frameworks.
Consequences: core product works with AI off; future providers sit behind bounded schemas/policies. Local-first remains approved.
Validation: dependency manifest excludes later-phase AI stack; no AI learning completion claimed in Phase 0.
